Atlas 210X VFO LCD mockup

RG1602A 16×2 drawn dot by dot (5×8 per character). Proposed layout. Real LCD contrast and glyphs may differ, so test on the real display first.

S6
The 8 custom character slots right now
  • Flags can use the LCD's built-in capitals or lowercase letters (no custom slots either way).
  • Row 1: mode (USB / LSB / TX), frequency, ! after an I²C fault, AGC F / S.
  • Row 2: PRE / ATT / blank, 9 S-meter fields (S1…S9), then +10…+40.
  • Odd fields show a small 3×6 digit; when the signal reaches the field it turns into the same digit cut out of a lit block. Even fields are bars with 3 dB half steps.
  • Small digits on both lit and unlit fields: each digit appears only once on screen, so its slot is rewritten when the field changes state. 5 digit slots + bar + half bar = 7 of 8.
