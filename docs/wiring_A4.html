A6/A7 have no internal pull-up → the external 10 k is required. One button at a time. Resistors 1 % or 5 % both fine.

4. Attenuator relay (D5) — RX path only

  • π-pad 100 / 68 / 100 Ω ≈ 10 dB (exact: 96 / 71 / 96 Ω). Relay bypasses or inserts it.
  • Only in the receive line after the antenna relay — never in the TX path. Short leads / thin coax.
  • 5 V relay: coil to +5 V instead of +12 V. Diode cathode (band) to + side.

5. AGC fast/slow (D13) — later

  • 2N7000: gate via 100 Ω from D13, 100 k gate–GND, source GND, drain via Rfast (~150–330 k, to try) to the AGC hold node on PC-300D.
  • Parts on PC-300D to be identified by value first (hold cap / 1 M discharge R).

6. MODE LSB/USB

Software only: BFO moved to the other side of the crystal filter (5.520.000 ↔ ~5.523.3 MHz, to be set) — LO calculation follows automatically.
