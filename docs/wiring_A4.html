4. ATT / NORM / PRE — ON-OFF-ON rocker, DPDT relays, RX only proposal

  • Coil high end on +12 V, the rocker switches the coil low end to GND (ATT or PRE, centre = NORM). The 1N4148 (band to the coil) passes the LOW to the Nano pin and blocks the +12 V when off.
  • DPDT signal relays: both poles switch the RF (in and out). π-pad 68 / 150 / 68 Ω ≈ 16 dB (20 dB: 62 / 240 / 62 Ω). Full board: preamp_bfg135.pdf.
  • Only in the receive line after the antenna relay — never in the TX path. Short leads / thin coax.

5. AGC fast/slow — DPDT rocker, sense D4 proposal

  • 2N7000 at PC-300D: gate via 100 Ω from the rocker (+12 V), 100 k gate–GND, source GND, drain via Rfast (~150–330 k, to try) to the AGC hold node on PC-300D.
  • Parts on PC-300D to be identified by value first (hold cap / 1 M discharge R).
  • Sense: DPDT rocker — 2nd pole common to GND, “fast” throw to D4 (INPUT_PULLUP, LOW = fast). No divider.

9. Calibrator — Si5351 CLK2 proposal

  • Thin coax from CLK2 to where the PC-820 100 kHz cal board fed the receiver input — run it now while the VFO box is open (injection point to be located), receiver end left open for now.
  • Inject weakly: 1–2 pF or 47 k in series at the injection point (marker about S9 or less).
  • CLK2 on only in S3 CAL (A7 sense) or from the menu — off otherwise (a running CLK2 can add spurs).
  • 100 kHz-style markers or an exact carrier (e.g. 14.100.000). With a GPSDO 1PPS the Nano can calibrate CORR automatically.

10. Data audio + PC PTT relay proposal

  • Out from PC-300 pin 22: after the AGC, before AF gain and VariBeam → level independent of the volume knob.
  • In at PC-200 pin 17 (mic input): the MIC gain knob sets the PC level. 47 k / 470 Ω = line to mic level.
  • DPDT relay (e.g. Finder 30.22, 16 mA) + flyback diode: PC PTT keys the radio and opens the mic line. Mic PTT unchanged.
  • PTT relay board wires: yellow = VariBeam D9, red = +12 V (through PTT ON/OFF), grey = GND, white = PTT to the KEY line.
  • Driver: VariBeam D9 → 4.7 k → BC547 base, 47 k base–GND, emitter GND, collector → coil low end. Coil 12 V DPDT, 930 Ω (≈ 13 mA); 1N4148 across the coil, band to +12 V.
  • PTT ON/OFF switch (SPST) in the coil +12 V feed: hard off for uploads / testing; ON for FT8 / data. TX timeout also in the VariBeam firmware.
  • VFO Nano D13 → 10 k → VariBeam D8: D8 HIGH ≥ 300 ms keys, LOW releases at once (D13 bootloader blinks ≈ 100 ms are ignored).
  • Transformers 600:600; PC side (pin 6) floating, radio side to the radio audio ground.
