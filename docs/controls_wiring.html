Atlas 210X — VFO Nano buttons, rockers, relays and sense wires

YT3CT · 2026-10 · proposal · diode band = cathode. Parts at the source end, plain wires to the Nano (5 V or less).
  • From the Nano to the front / relays: A6, GND, D4, D5, D6. A6 readings: STEP 0, MENU ≈ 184 (170 with MODE), MODE alone ≈ 700, nothing 1023. A2 goes to the KEY line, A7 to the S3 network. The +12 V comes from the radio, never from the Nano.
  • D5 / D6 / A2: Nano internal pull-up, LOW = on. D6 becomes PRE sense only after STEP has moved to A6. Relay contacts (RF in / out of the π-pad and preamp) are not shown.
  • A7: CW ≈ 3.8 V, CAL ≈ 2.1 V, REC / TRANS 0 V. Set CW_SENSE_FITTED 1 in the sketch once this network is wired.
