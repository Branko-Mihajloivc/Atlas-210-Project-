ATT / PRE board — relays, 10 dB attenuator, BFG135 preamp

YT3CT · 2026-10 · proposal · RX line between PC-800 OUT and PC-100 pin 1 (after the preselector). RX only — never in the TX path.
  • Relays at rest (both coils off) = NOR: signal goes IN → K1 bypass → K2 bypass → OUT.
  • ATT: K1 pulls, both poles move to NO → signal through the π-pad. PRE: K2 pulls → signal through the preamp. The rocker can only select one.
  • Coil high ends permanently +12 V; the rocker grounds the coil low end. Flyback 1N4148 across each coil (band to +12 V). Sense 1N4148 from the coil low end to D5 / D6 (band to the coil) — Nano reads LOW = on.
  • Thin coax (RG-174 / RG-316) in and out, short connections on the board, ground plane; keep the bypass track short and away from the preamp output.
  • Check before cutting: the PC-800 → PC-100 coax must carry receive only (no TX signal).

Layout

  • Double-sided, bottom = solid ground plane with many vias. RF tracks short and wide.
  • Signal flow in a line: J1 → K1 → K2 → J2; pad between the K1 poles, preamp between the K2 poles.
  • Preamp input and output apart (no loop); BFG135 tab on a copper area; ferrite bead right at the collector.
  • Coils, sense diodes and supply in a corner near J3, away from the RF path.
  • Coax shields to the ground plane right at J1 / J2.

Notes

  • Expected: ~10–11 dB gain, 50 Ω in / out, flat 3.5–30 MHz. More gain: RF 270 Ω + RE1 8.2 Ω (~12 dB).
  • Check after building: collector ≈ 9.6 V, emitter ≈ 1.6 V (= 50 mA). Adjust R2 if far off.
  • BFG135 has fT ≈ 7 GHz: build on a ground-plane board, very short leads, all ground returns to the plane.
  • Supply: permanent +12 V, or switched on with PRE (PNP from the PRE coil low end) to save ~60 mA.
  • On the PRE relay: pole 1 switches the input, pole 2 the output (bypass when off). RX only — never in the TX path.
  • Test with the tinySA (sweep through the amp, 50 Ω both ends) or by ear on 10 / 15 m.
