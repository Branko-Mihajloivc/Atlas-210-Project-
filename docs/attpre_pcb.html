ATT / PRE board — PCB plan for EasyEDA

48 × 60 mm, on the crystal filter can. IN top left → K2 PRE → K1 ATT → OUT top right. Preamp on the left (away from the Nano), pad on the right.

Design rules (home etching)

  • Parts 1206 (resistors, capacitors), BFG135 SOT-223, chokes 1210 or axial.
  • RF tracks 1.5 mm, power 2 mm, others ≥ 1 mm; clearance ≥ 0.6 mm.
  • Top: tracks + ground pour (0.8 mm clearance). Bottom: unetched solid copper = ground plane.
  • Top ground to bottom: wire pins through drilled holes, at least next to every GND pad of the RF parts, at J1 / J2 and around the preamp.
  • Relay holes: no relay pin is GND — clear the bottom copper (ø 2.5 mm) around every relay pin and every other non-GND through-hole.
  • Coil pins overhang the edge: flyback 1N4148 and sense diode soldered directly on the coil pins, wires to the rocker / Nano.
