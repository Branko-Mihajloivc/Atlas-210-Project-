Order of work

  1. After etching: beep every track against GND — no beep except the GND tracks.
  2. Drill: relay pins and coax / J3 pads 1.2 mm, braid pads 1.5 mm, the 11 ground holes (small circles at the GND pads) 0.9 mm.
  3. Bottom copper: countersink (ø 3 mm drill, by hand) every relay hole and the J1 / J2 / J3 core holes. Ground holes and braid holes stay connected.
  4. Ground wires: bare wire through each of the 11 ground holes, solder top and bottom.
  5. SMD flat parts first (resistors, capacitors), then L1, L2, FB1, then Q1 (tab soldered fully — heat).
  6. Relays last, from the bottom, raised ~1 mm; solder the pins on top. Coil pins stay outside the board: flyback and sense diodes on them, wires to the rocker / Nano.
  7. Coax: core to the round pad, braid flat onto the square pad (top), short pigtails.
